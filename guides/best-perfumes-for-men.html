The best perfumes for men: fresh, woody and warm picks

By Saffron & Still, Cheltenham · Perfume guide · November 2026

Choosing a men's perfume can feel hard. There are hundreds of bottles, and the notes can read like a shopping list. The good news is that most men's scents fall into a few simple styles. Find the style you like, and the choice gets much easier.

The main styles of men's perfume

Think of these as families. Most men's perfumes sit in one of them, or blend two together.

FreshCitrus, apple, mint and green notes. Clean and easy.
AromaticLavender, rosemary and geranium. Classic and smart.
Woody and spicyPepper, cedar, sandalwood and patchouli. Warm and confident.
Rich and warmOud, saffron, leather, amber and vanilla. Bold and cosy.
  • If he likes to smell clean, look for citrus, apple, mint or bergamot near the top.
  • If he wants something for work, go fresh or aromatic. They are easy to wear around other people.
  • If he likes going out, try woody and spicy. Pepper and sandalwood feel a bit more evening.
  • If he likes bold scents, look for oud, saffron, leather or amber in the base.

How to read the notes

Every perfume on our site lists top, heart and base notes. Here is what they mean.

  • Top notes are what you smell first. They are bright and fade within about half an hour.
  • Heart notes come next and shape the main character of the scent.
  • Base notes last the longest. They are what you smell on a jumper at the end of the day.

A tip: do not judge a perfume only on the first spray. The base is what he will live with most, so check it matches the style he likes.

Eau de toilette, eau de parfum or extrait?

You will see these words on every bottle. They tell you how strong the perfume is.

  • Eau de toilette is the lightest. It is fresh and easy, and good for daytime. Voyage Pour Homme and Bound by Sapil are both eau de toilette.
  • Eau de parfum is stronger and usually lasts longer. Blue by Ahmed Al Maghribi and Taraf Black are eau de parfum.
  • Extrait de parfum is the richest. It has the most perfume oil, so two sprays often last all day.

How to wear it

  • Spray on skin, not clothes. Neck, chest and wrists work best. Warm skin helps the scent open up.
  • Do not rub your wrists. It breaks down the top notes faster.
  • Moisturise first. An unscented lotion after a shower helps perfume stick to dry skin.
  • Fresh for day, warm for night. Many men keep two bottles: one easy, one bolder.

Common questions

What is the best perfume for a man who does not usually wear it?
Start fresh and light. Citrus, apple and green notes are easy to like and hard to overdo. Voyage Pour Homme or Bound by Sapil are good first bottles.
What is the difference between eau de toilette, eau de parfum and extrait?
It is about strength. Eau de toilette is the lightest, eau de parfum is in the middle, and extrait de parfum has the most perfume oil. Stronger usually means it lasts longer, so you need fewer sprays.
Can men wear unisex perfume?
Yes. Unisex simply means it was made for anyone. Lots of men wear unisex scents, especially woody, spicy and oud styles.
How many sprays should a man use?
Two to four sprays is plenty for most scents. With an extrait, start with two. You should notice it when someone leans in, not across the room.
