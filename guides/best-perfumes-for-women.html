The best perfumes for women: fresh, floral and sweet picks

By Saffron & Still, Cheltenham · Perfume guide · November 2026

Picking a women's perfume can be tricky. There are so many bottles, and the notes can read like a fruit bowl and a flower shop at once. The good news is that most women's scents fall into a few simple styles. Find the style you love, and the choice gets much easier.

The main styles of women's perfume

Think of these as families. Most perfumes sit in one of them, or blend two together.

Fresh and fruityCitrus, pear, lychee and berries. Bright and easy.
FloralRose, peony, jasmine and gardenia. Pretty and classic.
SweetVanilla, caramel, praline and marshmallow. Cosy and fun.
Warm and richAmber, sandalwood, patchouli and spice. Elegant and lasting.
  • If she likes to smell clean, look for bergamot, pear or lychee at the top and white musk in the base.
  • If she loves flowers, look for rose, peony, magnolia or jasmine in the heart.
  • If she has a sweet tooth, try scents with vanilla, caramel, praline or marshmallow.
  • If she likes something grown-up, look for amber, sandalwood, patchouli or saffron.

How to read the notes

Every perfume on our site lists top, heart and base notes. Here is what they mean.

  • Top notes are what you smell first. They are bright and fade within about half an hour.
  • Heart notes come next and shape the main character of the scent.
  • Base notes last the longest. They are what you smell on a scarf at the end of the day.

A tip: do not judge a perfume only on the first spray. The base is what she will live with most, so check it matches the style she likes.

Eau de parfum or extrait?

You will see these words on every bottle. They tell you how strong the perfume is.

  • Eau de parfum is the most common strength. It lasts well and is easy to wear. Taraf White, Mahib and Kiss are all eau de parfum.
  • Extrait de parfum is the richest. It has more perfume oil, so it smells deeper and sits closer to the skin. Timeless by Adyan and Glacia Frost are both extraits, so two sprays often go a long way.

Day or night?

There are no strict rules, but this helps.

  • For the office, pick fresh, fruity or clean floral scents. Taraf White and Mahib are easy to wear around other people.
  • For weekends, florals like Selene feel relaxed and pretty.
  • For evenings out, go sweeter or richer. Kiss, Timeless and Glacia Frost all have more presence.
  • For colder months, honey, amber and vanilla feel warm and cosy. Lily of Valencia is a good one.

How to wear it

  • Spray on skin. Neck, chest and wrists work best. Warm skin helps the scent open up.
  • Do not rub your wrists. It breaks down the top notes faster.
  • Moisturise first. An unscented lotion helps perfume stick to dry skin.
  • Less is more with extraits. Start with two sprays and add more only if you need to.

Common questions

What is a good first perfume for a woman who does not usually wear it?
Start with something fresh or softly floral. Fruity top notes like pear and lychee, with a clean musk base, are easy to like. Taraf White by Zimaya or Mahib by Adyan are good first bottles.
Can women wear unisex perfume?
Yes. Unisex simply means it was made for anyone. Many women love unisex oud, amber and woody scents.
What is the difference between eau de parfum and extrait de parfum?
It is about strength. Extrait de parfum has more perfume oil than eau de parfum, so it smells richer, sits closer to the skin and usually lasts longer. You need fewer sprays.
What perfume should I buy her if I do not know her taste?
Go for a soft fruity floral or a clean rose-musk. They suit most people. If she loves desserts or vanilla candles, try a sweet scent instead. Unopened bottles can be returned within 14 days.
