How to choose a perfume someone will love

  • Think about what they already wear. Sweet and cosy, fresh and clean, or dark and smoky? Pick from the same family.
  • Check the notes, not the name. If they love coffee, vanilla or citrus in food, they will often love it in perfume too.
  • Still unsure? A soft vanilla or a clean citrus is the safest gift. Oud is a bigger personality, so save it for someone who likes rich scents.
