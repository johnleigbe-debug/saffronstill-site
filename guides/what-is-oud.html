What is oud? A beginner's guide to oud perfume

What is oud? A beginner's guide to oud perfume

By Saffron & Still, Cheltenham · Perfume guide · November 2026

Oud is one of the most talked-about notes in perfume, and one of the most misunderstood. Here is what it is, what it smells like, and six oud perfumes to start with.

What is oud?

Oud (also called agarwood or aoud) comes from the heartwood of Aquilaria trees, which grow in South and Southeast Asia. When the tree is infected by a type of mould, it produces a dark, fragrant resin to protect itself. That resin-soaked wood is oud.

Wild oud is rare and slow to form, which is why pure oud oil is one of the most expensive perfume materials in the world. Most modern oud perfumes use an oud accord, a blend that recreates the smell, often mixed with natural materials.

WoodyDry, deep wood like a cedar chest
SmokyA soft, incense-like smoke
LeatherySome ouds feel like a worn leather jacket
SweetRich and almost honeyed when blended

What does oud smell like?

On its own, oud is strong: woody, smoky, slightly animalic and very long-lasting. In most perfumes it is blended with other notes that change its character:

  • Rose and saffron make it warm and glowing, the classic Arabian pairing.
  • Vanilla, caramel and praline make it sweet and cosy.
  • Fruit like blackcurrant or peach make it softer and easier for beginners.
  • Leather and pepper make it bold and dramatic.

How to wear oud

  • Start small. One or two sprays is enough. Oud is strong and grows on the skin.
  • Spray on skin, not clothes. Oud develops with your body heat over a few hours.
  • Save the bold ones for evenings and winter. Sweeter, fruitier ouds work for daytime.

Common questions

Is oud for men or women?
Both. Oud is traditionally worn by everyone in the Middle East, and most oud perfumes are unisex.
Why is oud so expensive?
Natural oud forms slowly and only in infected Aquilaria trees, so real oud oil is rare. Most perfumes use an oud accord to keep the price reasonable.
How long does oud perfume last?
Oud is one of the longest-lasting notes in perfume. A good oud perfume often lasts all day on skin, and longer on clothes.
Which oud is best for a beginner?
Start with a sweet or fruity oud such as Mountain of Oud or Secret Oud. They are softer than a pure, smoky oud.
