Our story

Two things we love: scent and spirit

Saffron & Still grew out of a simple enthusiasm for beautiful scent and for spirits made with real spices.

Fragrance. Our collection is chosen bottle by bottle: niche Eau de Parfum and Extrait from houses across the Middle East and beyond, from rich ouds and ambers to fresh citrus and sweet gourmands. Every bottle is brand new and unused.

Spice. Cardamom, star anise, clove, cinnamon, nutmeg and pepper are the thread that ties our perfumes and our spirits together.

Spirit. Our Masala gin, vodka, rum and negroni are small-batch spirits infused with traditional Indian spices. They are on the way.

We are a small independent shop based in Cheltenham.
